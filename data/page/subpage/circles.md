## Circles of Light

Come as you are. There is nothing to do and nothing to fix.

Wednesdays 20:00 and Fridays 18:00, Lisbon time. About 1 to 1½ hours.

€22 a circle. If €22 is hard for you right now, write to Tiago. He would rather you come.

### How to book

First, choose your circle on the [booking page](https://circlesoflight-bookings.vercel.app/).

Then Tiago confirms your place and emails you a private video link within 12 hours.

Join a few minutes early, lie down or sit somewhere quiet, and settle. Camera on or off is your choice. Nothing is recorded.
