## Questions, answered simply.

### What is a Circle of Light?

A quiet gathering where you lie down, feel at ease and open to the healing energy. There is nothing to do and nothing to fix.

### Do I need to believe anything?

No. An open, respectful heart is enough. Everyone is welcome.

### When are the circles?

Wednesdays 20:00 and Fridays 18:00, Lisbon time. About 1 to 1½ hours.

### How much does it cost?

€22 a circle. The 11.11 opening circle is €11. A 1:1 is €88. If €22 is hard right now, write to Tiago.

### How do I book?

Use the [booking page](https://circlesoflight-bookings.vercel.app/). Tiago confirms your place and emails you a private video link within 12 hours.

### Online or in person?

Online today, from anywhere in the world. In-person circles in Kos Town are coming; dates and place will be shared soon.

### What will I feel?

Everyone's experience is different. Some people feel warmth or rest, some fall asleep, some notice little. There is no right way to receive.

### Is it recorded?

No. Camera on or off is your choice.

### Can I change my date?

Free reschedule up to 24 hours before. No cash refunds.
